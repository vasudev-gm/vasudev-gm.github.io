### Quick Report

NVIDIA raised the price of Shield TV Pro from $199.99 to $299.99 on October 2 and says it has stopped producing the standard tube-shaped Shield TV. The company cited rising component costs, including memory, and the Pro is now the only Shield model in production.
<!-- more -->

The hardware has not changed: the Pro still uses a Tegra X1+ with 3 GB of RAM and 16 GB of storage, and NVIDIA has not announced a successor. The $100 increase follows nearly seven years at the original price, but leaves prospective buyers paying more for the same device.
