### Quick Report

Samsung and SK hynix are reportedly moving toward licensing YMTC\'s Xtacking NAND architecture. Xtacking separates the memory array from its peripheral logic so that each side can be optimized more independently, potentially improving speed and density.
<!-- more -->

The arrangement could spread a valuable 3D-NAND design approach across more of the global supply chain, although implementation details and royalty terms remain key commercial questions. It also shows that leading memory manufacturers continue to rely on cross-industry technology access rather than treating every innovation as fully proprietary.

For the NAND market, licensing could accelerate mature versions of the architecture or create different derivatives among suppliers. The real effect will depend on yields, node scaling, and whether the technology reaches competitive SSD and enterprise products.
