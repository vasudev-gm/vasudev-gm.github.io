### Quick Report

Qualcomm has introduced Snapdragon C-powered laptops priced around $300. The company says the new systems can outperform Intel’s N250 processor by up to 67 percent, bringing its Arm-based PC platform to a more price-sensitive segment.
<!-- more -->

Affordable laptops will need to balance performance with battery life, software compatibility, and device quality. Qualcomm’s results will also depend on how broadly Windows applications and OEM designs support the Snapdragon C platform.
