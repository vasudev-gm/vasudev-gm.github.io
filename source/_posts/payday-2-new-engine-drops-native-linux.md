### Quick Report

Payday 2 is receiving a new engine 13 years after its original launch, but the transition will also end official native Linux support. The change gives the long-running cooperative shooter a modern technical foundation while altering how Linux players access the game.
<!-- more -->

Players on Linux may need to rely on compatibility layers or other workarounds after the engine migration. The update’s effect on performance, mod support, existing content, and platform compatibility will depend on the final implementation and developer support.
