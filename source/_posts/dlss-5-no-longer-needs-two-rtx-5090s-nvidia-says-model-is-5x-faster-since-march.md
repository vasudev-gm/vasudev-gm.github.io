### Quick Report

NVIDIA says its DLSS 5 model is now roughly five times faster than it was when first shown in March, and it no longer requires a dual-RTX 5090 setup to run. The company says the model now works across the RTX 50-series stack, from the RTX 5060 up through the RTX 5090.
<!-- more -->

The update is meaningful because DLSS 5 still imposes a noticeable performance tax versus native rendering, but the acceleration gains show that the feature is rapidly maturing. For the first time, the roadmap no longer looks like a theoretical benchmark artifact confined to the highest-end hardware.
