### Quick Report

UGREEN has launched the LinkStation Pro eGPU dock at a reported $450 price, combining Thunderbolt 5 connectivity with an integrated 850 W power supply. The product is designed to add desktop-class graphics capability to compatible laptops and compact PCs through a single external enclosure.
<!-- more -->

The integrated power budget should support demanding graphics cards while reducing the need for a separate external PSU. Thunderbolt 5 gives the dock a higher-bandwidth connection than earlier eGPU enclosures, although real-world performance will still depend on the host system, graphics card, driver support, and the workload. Regional availability and the complete port specification may vary.
