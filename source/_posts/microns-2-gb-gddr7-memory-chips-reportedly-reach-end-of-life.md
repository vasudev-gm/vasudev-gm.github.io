### Quick Report

Micron appears to have retired its 2 GB GDDR7 memory dies, a sign that the market may be moving away from the smaller package size that briefly emerged for RTX 50-class designs. The move matters because those chips were used in some early high-end graphics cards, and their discontinuation points to a broader transition toward larger, more practical memory footprints.
<!-- more -->

The concern is not immediate product chaos, but planning pressure. Samsung and SK Hynix still dominate most of the supply base, and Micron was never the primary source for the latest Nvidia graphics cards. Even so, the disappearance of the 2 GB option suggests vendors are increasingly favoring 3 GB or larger modules, which could complicate board design and push costs higher as 2027 GPU roadmaps take shape.
