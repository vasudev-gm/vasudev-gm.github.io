### Quick Report

Kojima Productions says PlayStation canceled the in-development Physint project in mid-June 2026, and the studio is now moving forward with Xbox as a partner. Hideo Kojima said the team searched for a new publisher after the cancellation, ultimately landing on a partnership that keeps the project alive.
