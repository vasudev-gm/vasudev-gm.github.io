### Quick Report

Ubuntu has outlined an AI desktop integration centered on Warty, a launcher-style entry point for assistance across apps and local context. The project is designed to be optional, with AI capabilities enabled only when users ask for them.
<!-- more -->

Ubuntu says its priorities are local inference, privacy, and security through isolation and sandboxing; the default installation will not include large AI models. The team expects to demonstrate more of the integration and provide an installable version of Warty in January 2027.
