### Quick Report

Marvell has introduced the Bravera SC6 SSD controller for PCIe Gen 6 NVMe SSDs. The controller is aimed at storage products that need the bandwidth and throughput potential of the newer PCI Express standard.
<!-- more -->

PCIe Gen 6 storage will require coordinated support across NAND, controllers, host platforms, and software. Enterprise deployments will also evaluate latency, power efficiency, reliability features, and the ability to maintain performance under sustained workloads.
