### Quick Report

ASRock has issued a new Radeon RX 7900 XT/XTX vBIOS update, but the rollout has triggered concern because some users report that flashing the update can brick their graphics cards. The issue is a sharp reminder that firmware updates for high-end GPUs are not always low-risk, especially when vendors push patch files across multiple variants at once.
<!-- more -->

The broader problem is that board partners are increasingly juggling security fixes, performance tweaks, and compatibility updates in a single BIOS package. When a firmware file fails or a card is flashed with the wrong variant, the outcome can be permanent, and the damage is felt immediately by owners who depend on their GPUs for work or gaming.

For enthusiasts, the lesson is simple: BIOS updates need to be treated like system-critical maintenance, not routine housekeeping. Until vendors provide clearer change logs and more reliable flash procedures, the reliability risk around GPU firmware will remain high even for expensive enthusiast-class cards.
