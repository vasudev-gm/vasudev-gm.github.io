### Quick Report

Xbox disc-to-digital conversion is arriving this month for Xbox One and Xbox Series X. The feature gives eligible players a way to associate supported disc-based games with digital licenses on their console.
<!-- more -->

The change could make backward-compatible libraries more convenient while reducing the need to insert a disc for later play sessions. Availability and eligibility will depend on the games supported by Microsoft and the ownership rules applied to each title.
