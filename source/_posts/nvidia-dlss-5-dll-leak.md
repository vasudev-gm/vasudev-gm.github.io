### Quick Report

A DLSS Neural Rendering DLL found in the early-access build of NBA 2K27 may offer an early clue about NVIDIA DLSS 5 availability. The file is about 158 MB, substantially larger than the 72 MB DLSS 4.5 Ray Reconstruction DLL, and NVIDIA is expected to launch its neural-rendering technology in fall 2026.
<!-- more -->

The larger package may contain a generative model intended to improve lighting, materials, and visual realism while preserving scene identity, character poses, motion, and artistic direction. The file cannot currently be activated in NBA 2K27 because the game lacks the required controls and engine integration, so neither NVIDIA nor 2K has confirmed that the title will ship with working DLSS 5 support.
