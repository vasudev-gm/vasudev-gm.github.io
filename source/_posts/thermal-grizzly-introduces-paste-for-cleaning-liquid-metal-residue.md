### Quick Report

Thermal Grizzly says its TG Metal Polish & Restore is formulated to remove gallium residue left by liquid-metal compounds and clean tarnished metal surfaces. The company lists copper, brass, chrome, and nickel among its intended materials, including CPU dies and heat spreaders.
<!-- more -->

The $22.11 kit includes lint-free microfiber cloths and a polishing pen for applying the paste; the company says the formula also leaves temporary oxidation protection. It is intended for cleaning and surface treatment, not as a thermal interface material between the processor and cooler.
