### Quick Report

New Linux CPU driver patches reportedly reduce game stuttering on the Steam Deck. The work targets low-level processor behavior, where scheduling and power-management decisions can affect frame pacing even when average performance appears sufficient.
<!-- more -->

Better CPU behavior could make games feel smoother without requiring higher average frame rates or major hardware changes. The patches still need to reach widely used kernels and SteamOS builds before most players see the benefit automatically.
