### Quick Report

Samsung has unveiled the P9 and P7 portable SSDs at Gamescom 2026, bringing USB4 connectivity to a new storage lineup. The flagship P9 reaches sequential read speeds of 4,000 MB/s and write speeds of 3,800 MB/s, roughly twice the performance of the previous generation, and is available in capacities up to 8 TB.
<!-- more -->

The P9 is designed for professional workflows such as 12K direct recording and large RAW or high-resolution video files, while the P7 targets everyday storage across PCs, smartphones, consoles, and cameras. Both models will launch globally from August 31 in 1 TB, 2 TB, 4 TB, and 8 TB capacities, with P9 prices ranging from $339.99 to $2,699.99 and P7 prices from $289.99 to $2,289.99.
