### Quick Report

Corsair launched an updated 1200 W RM1200e power supply with PinProtect, its per-pin overcurrent protection for the native 12V-2x6 GPU connection. Corsair says the feature monitors all six 12 V pins and reduces power if one remains above its preset current threshold, triggering a protective GPU shutdown.
<!-- more -->

The PSU is Cybenetics Platinum certified, reaches up to 91% overall efficiency, and carries Intel ATX 3.1 and PCIe 5.1 certifications plus a 10-year warranty. Corsair lists launch pricing from US$204.99 in the United States; the feature and price details come from the manufacturer\'s announcement.
