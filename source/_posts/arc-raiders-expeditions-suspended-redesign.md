### Quick Report

Embark has suspended Expeditions in ARC Raiders after community backlash and will redesign the feature before bringing it back.
<!-- more -->

The decision gives the studio time to reassess how Expeditions fit into the game\'s progression and player experience. A redesigned return will likely depend on addressing the concerns that prompted the backlash while preserving the feature\'s intended role in the live-service game.
