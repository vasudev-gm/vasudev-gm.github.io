### Quick Report

ASUS ROG has quietly launched a USB dongle with 10 Gbps Ethernet connectivity. The accessory gives compatible desktops and laptops a way to add high-speed wired networking without installing an internal network card.
<!-- more -->

A USB adapter can be useful for compact systems, notebooks, and motherboards with limited expansion options. Real-world throughput will depend on the USB connection, driver support, thermal behavior, and the rest of the network equipment.
