### Quick Report

Rockstar is reported to have shut down Leonida, described as the largest community effort to recompile GTA 6 for PC use. The project aimed to adapt or reconstruct large parts of the game outside an official release path.
<!-- more -->

Recompilation projects can sit outside legitimate modding because they may duplicate protected code or assets, and they can expose unfinished or unauthorized game material. A takedown is consistent with publishers protecting proprietary builds and distribution rights.

The action may slow the specific project, but it does not eliminate all legitimate community work around GTA 6. Official PC support and authorized mods remain separate issues from rebuilding or distributing an existing executable.
