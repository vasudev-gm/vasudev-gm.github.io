### Quick Report

Desktop graphics card shipments hit 12.5 million units in Q2 2026, the highest quarterly total in years, according to research from Jon Peddie. The figure represents a 5.9% increase over the prior quarter and a 7.8% rise compared with the same period a year earlier, even as GPU prices continued to climb.
<!-- more -->

The data points to strong end-user demand despite higher pricing and ongoing memory shortages. It also suggests the market is being driven by a combination of replacement demand, the pushing of older cards to their limits, and a continued desire to buy before future tariff or memory-related price spikes. That makes the quarter stand out as an unusually strong buyer market even in a period of widespread cost inflation.
