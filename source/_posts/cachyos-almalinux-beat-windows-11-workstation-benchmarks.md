### Quick Report

CachyOS and AlmaLinux have reportedly beaten Windows 11 in workstation benchmarks across selected workloads. The results highlight how operating-system configuration, kernel behavior, drivers, and application support can influence performance on the same hardware.
<!-- more -->

Benchmark outcomes do not represent every workload or system configuration, so users should consider the applications they actually run. Linux distributions can deliver strong results in optimized workloads, while Windows may retain advantages in software compatibility and support.
