### Quick Report

TCL CSOT has shown a display prototype capable of a 1250 Hz refresh rate while maintaining full QHD resolution. The figure is far beyond the refresh rates used by current gaming and professional displays and demonstrates how display drive electronics can push panel performance.
<!-- more -->

The prototype\'s extreme refresh rate could benefit esports, simulation, and high-speed visual work, although panel brightness, response behavior, color quality, and practical input pathways will determine whether the demo becomes a commercial product. Refresh rate is only one part of display responsiveness.

For now, the demonstration is a technology signal rather than a confirmed retail specification. It shows that panel makers are pursuing higher temporal resolution as one way to differentiate future monitors, even if the first implementation remains highly specialized.
