### Quick Report

Microsoft is reportedly deleting a 32 GB RAM recommendation from its Windows 11 guidance. The change could reduce confusion for users whose systems have less memory, although the amount of RAM needed still depends on applications, multitasking, and other workload demands.
<!-- more -->

Removing a recommendation does not necessarily change Windows 11’s formal minimum requirements or guarantee identical performance across different systems. Users should continue to consider their software, browser usage, gaming needs, and upgrade plans when choosing a memory capacity.
