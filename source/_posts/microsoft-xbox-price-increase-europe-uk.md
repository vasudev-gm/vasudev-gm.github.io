### Quick Report

Microsoft is raising Xbox prices by up to 43 percent in Europe and the UK. The increase makes Xbox hardware more expensive for regional buyers and could change the value comparison between consoles, subscriptions, and gaming PCs.
<!-- more -->

The size of the adjustment may affect demand for current Xbox models and the company position in price-sensitive markets. Buyers may also compare the revised prices with PlayStation hardware and other ways to access Xbox games.
