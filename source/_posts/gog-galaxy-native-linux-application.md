### Quick Report

GOG is preparing a native Linux application for its GOG Galaxy client. The company\'s hiring and development direction indicate that Linux support is becoming a larger part of Galaxy\'s future rather than relying exclusively on compatibility layers or third-party launchers.
<!-- more -->

Native support could give Linux users a more integrated way to manage GOG libraries, updates, cloud saves, and related platform features. GOG has not yet provided a complete launch schedule, so the scope of the first release remains unclear.
