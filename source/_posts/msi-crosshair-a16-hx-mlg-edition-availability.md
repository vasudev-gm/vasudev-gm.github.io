### Quick Report

MSI has announced availability of the Crosshair A16 HX MLG Edition gaming laptop. The special-edition notebook combines the Crosshair platform with an MLG-themed design for PC gamers looking for a distinctive system.
<!-- more -->

The laptop will compete on performance, cooling, display quality, and portability alongside other gaming notebooks. Pricing and the exact hardware configuration will determine how attractive the MLG Edition is compared with standard Crosshair A16 HX models.
