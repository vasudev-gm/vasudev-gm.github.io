### Quick Report

Google has launched the Pixel 11 series, bringing its new Tensor G6 chip to the company’s latest smartphones. The new processor continues Google’s strategy of combining custom silicon with Android and Pixel-specific computational photography and artificial intelligence features.
<!-- more -->

The series gives Google a new hardware platform for improving camera processing, on-device AI, and power management. Final differences between models, regional availability, and long-term performance will depend on each device’s configuration and software support.
