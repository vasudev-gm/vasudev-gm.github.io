### Quick Report

Reports suggest GTA VI could require a newer PlayStation 5 firmware revision, potentially leaving current jailbreak users behind as Sony continues to tighten platform protections. The issue is not just about convenience; it is also a reminder that console security, anti-cheat tooling, and system software updates are now deeply tied to game compatibility.

The core concern is that newer games can quietly lock themselves to the current firmware baseline, especially when the platform vendor needs to harden systems against tampering or unauthorized runtime modifications. For players who rely on jailbroken or modified consoles, that can mean a game becomes effectively unavailable unless they move to a supported software state.

Even with the reports still unofficial, the pattern is familiar. Security updates and game support often advance together on closed ecosystems, and the effect is that system flexibility drops even when the underlying hardware is the same. That makes the real story less about one title and more about how much control the platform owner retains over the software stack.
