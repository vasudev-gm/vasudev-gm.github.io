### Quick Report

A 28-core Intel Nova Lake-S processor has surfaced in testing with a reported clock of 5.2 GHz. The appearance gives a first signal for the desktop processor\'s core layout and potential frequency range, but engineering samples are not a confirmation of final retail specifications.
<!-- more -->

Nova Lake-S is expected to test a more varied mix of performance and efficiency cores across high-end desktop configurations. The 28-core count is most useful as a clue to the architecture\'s segmentation because Intel can still change cache, clocks, power limits, and core ratios before production samples ship.

The 5.2 GHz figure should also be viewed as a test-environment result. Final performance will depend on sustained all-core behavior, thermal headroom, and how the platform and memory configuration are validated.
