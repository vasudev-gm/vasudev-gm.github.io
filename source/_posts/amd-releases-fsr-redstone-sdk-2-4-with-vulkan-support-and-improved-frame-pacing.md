AMD has released the FSR Redstone SDK 2.4, bringing Vulkan support and improvements to frame pacing as part of a broader effort to refine its upscaling software stack. The update is aimed at making FSR feel more consistent in a wider range of games and hardware setups.

The addition of Vulkan support matters because it broadens compatibility and can improve adoption across more engines and platforms. Improved frame pacing also matters just as much as raw throughput, since smoother animation and better temporal consistency often have a more noticeable effect on perceived quality than a headline percentage gain alone.
