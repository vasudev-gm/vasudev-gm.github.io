### Quick Report

Valve has formally moved the Index toward the end of its life, with the remaining stock now being sold at a reduced price and the headset marked as discontinued. For a product that originally launched at a premium, the move is a clear indication that Valve is shifting attention to newer hardware and that the lighthouse-based ecosystem is no longer the center of the company\'s current VR strategy.
<!-- more -->

The bigger story is not just the lower price tag. The Valve Index remains a high-quality headset for owners of existing base stations, but its long-term future is constrained by support realities, compatibility gaps, and the fact that Valve\'s new direction is built around different hardware assumptions. In other words, the clearance is a practical sell-through, not a revival of the old platform, and it highlights how quickly even enthusiast-focused VR gear can become legacy hardware in a market that still struggles to reach broad mainstream adoption.
