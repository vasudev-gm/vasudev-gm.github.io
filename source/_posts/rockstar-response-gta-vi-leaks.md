### Quick Report

Rockstar Games has publicly addressed the recent Grand Theft Auto VI leaks after legal action against alleged leakers failed to stop the material from spreading. The studio called the leaked gameplay heartbreaking, apologized for the extended development timeline, and asked players to wait until the planned November 19 release to experience the game properly.
<!-- more -->

The leaked material reportedly exposed gameplay systems, the map, vehicle designs, and NPC interactions from what may be an older build. Rockstar said it was preparing an extended official look at the game, while the continued attention around the leaks is expected to influence gaming hardware, software, and consumer spending ahead of the launch.
