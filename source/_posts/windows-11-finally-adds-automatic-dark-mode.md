### Quick Report

Windows 11 is finally gaining an automatic dark mode, giving the operating system a built-in way to switch between light and dark appearances without requiring users to change the setting manually. The feature addresses a long-standing convenience gap in the Windows personalization experience.
<!-- more -->

Automatic switching is useful for people who prefer a bright interface during the day and a darker one at night, especially on displays used in dim environments. The quality of the rollout will depend on how consistently Windows applies the schedule across system surfaces and applications, but the addition should remove one of the more noticeable small annoyances in everyday Windows use.
