### Quick Report

Microsoft appears to be pulling Copilot branding back from its newest Surface PCs, suggesting a more restrained approach to AI marketing in the hardware line. The change fits a broader pattern in which AI features remain central to the product story but are being presented with less attention-grabbing branding.
<!-- more -->

The practical effect is subtle but meaningful: the devices still carry AI capability, but the visible positioning shifts away from a dedicated Copilot identity and toward mainstream productivity messaging. That can help avoid overexposure while keeping the feature set in place for buyers who care about local AI assistance and app integration.

The broader takeaway is that the AI-PC narrative is maturing. Hardware makers are learning that users are less interested in loud branding and more interested in whether the features are useful, reliable, and clearly integrated into everyday workflows.
