### Quick Report

The upcoming Windows 11 26H2 release is expected to focus on support-cycle changes rather than a large collection of new features. Devices already running Windows 11 24H2 or 25H2 will reportedly receive 26H2 through a small enablement package, while older branches will require a full feature update.
<!-- more -->

Home and Pro editions are expected to receive 24 months of regular support, while Enterprise and Education editions receive 36 months. Microsoft is reserving a more substantial platform-wide release for Windows 11 27H2, while Windows 11 26H1 remains the separate track for newer Arm systems and upcoming NVIDIA RTX Spark devices.
