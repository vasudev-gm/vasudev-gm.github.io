### Quick Report

NVIDIA has reportedly secured TSMC’s A16 process for its next-generation Feynman GPUs. The arrangement would connect NVIDIA’s future architecture to TSMC’s advanced process technology as the company continues developing higher-performance accelerators.
<!-- more -->

An advanced node could improve performance, efficiency, or transistor density, but the final benefits will depend on the GPU design, packaging, memory system, and production schedule. The reported agreement also underscores the importance of securing leading-edge foundry capacity for AI hardware.
