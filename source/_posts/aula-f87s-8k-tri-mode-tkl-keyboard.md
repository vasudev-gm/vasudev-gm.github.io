### Quick Report

Aula has released its F87S series of tenkeyless mechanical gaming keyboards, combining 8K polling with tri-mode connectivity for wired, wireless, and Bluetooth use.
<!-- more -->

The compact TKL layout removes the number pad while retaining the main navigation and function keys. High polling and multiple connection options are aimed at players who want low-latency desktop use without giving up flexibility across devices.
