### Quick Report

Chinese DRAM maker CXMT has crossed the 9,000 MT/s barrier on DDR5, with a Colorful iGame X870E VULCAN W OC board and a 48 GB memory kit hitting speeds above 9,014 MT/s. The result is a meaningful milestone for a company that has been steadily closing the gap with the top three DRAM manufacturers in both yield and speed.
<!-- more -->

The new milestone follows earlier reports that CXMT was already nearing 90% DDR5 yield on a 17 nm node and testing latency improvements on ASUS and Colorful boards. That combination of increasingly mature manufacturing and high-speed memory tuning suggests CXMT is no longer just a low-cost alternative, but a realistic competitive player that could keep pressure on Samsung, SK hynix, and Micron as supply and pricing dynamics evolve.
