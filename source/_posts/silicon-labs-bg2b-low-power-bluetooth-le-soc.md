### Quick Report

Silicon Labs has unveiled the BG2B, its lowest-power Bluetooth Low Energy system-on-chip. The device targets connected products that need wireless functionality while keeping energy use low.
<!-- more -->

Lower power consumption can extend battery life in sensors, accessories, and other compact Internet of Things devices. Product designers will also weigh wireless range, memory, development support, and the cost of moving to the new platform.
