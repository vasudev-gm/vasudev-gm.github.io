### Quick Report

Apple CEO John Ternus is reportedly pushing for a faster product-development rhythm and a smaller, more experimental organization. Bloomberg reporting says the former hardware chief wants Apple to move beyond its familiar spring and fall launch pattern, potentially putting products on the market at shorter intervals.
<!-- more -->

The change is already being felt inside hardware engineering, where Apple has reportedly removed several program-management roles and around half a dozen directors. The company is also said to be reviewing how much work can be handled by AI support agents, although an earlier plan involving roughly 5,000 AppleCare jobs was reportedly abandoned. Apple has not publicly outlined a restructuring timetable, and employees are said to expect the transition to take years rather than happen in a single cycle.
