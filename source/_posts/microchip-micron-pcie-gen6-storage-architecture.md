### Quick Report

Microchip is partnering with Micron to showcase a high-performance PCIe Gen 6 storage architecture. The demonstration combines next-generation connectivity and storage components for demanding enterprise workloads.
<!-- more -->

PCIe Gen 6 can increase bandwidth between host systems and storage, but extracting that benefit requires compatible controllers, SSDs, platforms, and software. The architecture is aimed at the data movement demands of artificial intelligence and other bandwidth-intensive applications.
