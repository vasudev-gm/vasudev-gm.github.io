### Quick Report

Socionext is broadening its system-on-chip roadmap with Intel 18A-P process technology. The move gives the chip designer another advanced manufacturing option for future custom and application-specific silicon.
<!-- more -->

Using an advanced foundry process can help SoC designers target improvements in performance, efficiency, and integration. Actual product benefits will depend on the designs Socionext develops, manufacturing readiness, and customer adoption.
