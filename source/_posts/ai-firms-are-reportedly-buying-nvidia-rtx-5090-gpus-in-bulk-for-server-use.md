### Quick Report

Photos shared by HKEPC reportedly show pallets of retail GeForce RTX 5090 graphics cards being unpacked at AI server assembly sites. The cards are standard gaming models rather than RTX PRO 6000 or dedicated data-center accelerators, suggesting that AI firms may be competing directly with gamers for already scarce inventory.

The evidence should be treated cautiously because some images appear edited or AI-generated, but retail RTX 5090 pricing has reportedly climbed beyond $5,000 in some markets, far above its $1,999 launch price. Unlike China\'s RTX 5090D variants, the standard model has not been deliberately restricted for AI workloads, leaving open the possibility that server demand will continue to affect consumer supply and pricing.
