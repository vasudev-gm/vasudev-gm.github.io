### Quick Report

Intel Bartlett Lake-S processors are reportedly booting natively on MSI Z690 and Z790 motherboards. The compatibility would allow more workstation or enthusiast systems to use the processors without a board redesign or an adapter.
<!-- more -->

Native support depends on BIOS firmware, CPU identification, and the board\'s power-delivery and feature set. A successful boot does not automatically confirm full production support or compatibility across every Z690 and Z790 model.

The reports could be useful for users and system builders who want to keep existing 600- or 700-series boards. MSI or Intel would still need to document which firmware revisions and board models support Bartlett Lake-S before it becomes a dependable platform option.
