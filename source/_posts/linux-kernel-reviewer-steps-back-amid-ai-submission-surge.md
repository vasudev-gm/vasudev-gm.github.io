### Quick Report

Lorenzo Stoakes, a longtime Linux kernel contributor who reviews memory-management code, is stepping away from several review responsibilities after a growing volume of AI-generated submissions overwhelmed his available time. He says he can no longer reliably review work outside the areas he directly maintains.
<!-- more -->

The change affects reviews of memory-management patches and Rust code in that part of the kernel. Stoakes has reorganized a patch series so that relevant portions can go to other maintainers, while also describing the workload as difficult to manage amid the current pace of submissions.

The episode highlights a practical problem for open-source projects: faster code generation does not automatically create more review capacity. Maintainers still need to assess correctness, fit, and long-term maintenance costs, and an influx of low-context patches can consume time even when individual submissions are not harmful.
