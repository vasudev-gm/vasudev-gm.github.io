### Quick Report

NVIDIA’s A100 accelerator is expected to remain in use until 2029, six years after its original launch. The Ampere-generation GPU continues to serve data-center and artificial intelligence workloads where established software support and available infrastructure remain important.
<!-- more -->

Continued deployment shows that accelerator demand is not limited to the newest generation, particularly when customers prioritize validated platforms and predictable operating costs. Newer NVIDIA products may offer higher performance, but replacing existing A100 systems also involves software migration, power, and capital expenses.
