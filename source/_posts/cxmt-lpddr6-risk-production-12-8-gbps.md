### Quick Report

CXMT has entered LPDDR6 risk production with memory chips targeting data rates of up to 12.8 Gbps. The milestone indicates progress toward a newer low-power memory generation for mobile and other compact computing products.
<!-- more -->

Risk production is an early manufacturing stage used to validate a process and product before wider volume output. Final availability, yields, power behavior, and device qualification will determine how quickly LPDDR6 products can reach commercial systems.
