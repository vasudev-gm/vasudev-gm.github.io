### Quick Report

NVM Express has published a set of NVMe specifications focused on security, manageability, and sustainability. The updates extend the standards work beyond raw storage performance and address broader deployment requirements.
<!-- more -->

Standardized capabilities can make it easier for system builders and data-center operators to manage storage across products from different vendors. Adoption will depend on controller support, firmware implementation, host software, and the priorities of enterprise customers.
