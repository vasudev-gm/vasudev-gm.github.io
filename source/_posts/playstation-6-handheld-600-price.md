### Quick Report

A rumored USD 600 price target for a PlayStation 6 handheld appears difficult to achieve given the performance, battery, display, and manufacturing costs expected from a modern portable console.
<!-- more -->

The final price would depend on Sony\'s hardware choices, software strategy, and willingness to subsidize the device. A lower-cost design could improve accessibility, but it would also require careful compromises to performance and features compared with a full next-generation console.
