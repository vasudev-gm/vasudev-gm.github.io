### Quick Report

TSMC is discussing a possible joint venture with the Terafab project led by Elon Musk, according to a report later acknowledged by Musk. The proposed arrangement could see TSMC develop manufacturing capacity while Terafab contributes financing, equity, and purchase commitments; neither company has set out its final role publicly.
<!-- more -->

Terafab has also been linked to Intel, so a TSMC role would add another source of process expertise rather than establish a settled manufacturing plan. The report notes that equipment procurement and fab construction take years, and it remains uncertain which technologies TSMC would bring to a US project.
