### Quick Report

Microsoft says revenue from Windows OEM and devices fell by 22 percent, a sharp decline that reflects a rougher market for new hardware and a weaker pull-through from PC shipments. The drop is another reminder that even a strong software business can be dragged down by broader device demand.
<!-- more -->

The decline is not just a snapshot of one quarter but a signal that the PC market is still navigating weak demand, softer refresh cycles, and a more selective buying environment. For the industry, the result is slower hardware momentum even as AI and productivity initiatives remain a key strategic focus.
