### Quick Report

Samsung Foundry is reportedly preparing price hikes after a massive spike in demand for semiconductor manufacturing capacity. Strong demand from AI and other advanced-computing markets is increasing pressure on foundry availability and pricing.
<!-- more -->

Higher wafer prices could affect chip designers’ budgets, product pricing, and decisions about process nodes or manufacturing partners. The eventual impact will depend on contract terms, capacity expansion, customer demand, and competition among foundries.
