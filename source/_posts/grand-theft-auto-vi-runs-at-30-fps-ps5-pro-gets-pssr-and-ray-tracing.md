### Quick Report

Rockstar has confirmed that Grand Theft Auto VI will run at 30 frames per second on consoles, while the PlayStation 5 Pro will get its own extra visual treatment through PSSR and ray-traced effects. The base PS5 version will still be a premium title, but the Pro model is clearly being positioned as the sharper, more advanced console experience.
<!-- more -->

The key point is that 30 FPS is still the target for the mainline console version, which is unsurprising for a game of this scale but still a disappointment for players expecting a more modern framerate baseline. The PS5 Pro’s PSSR and ray-tracing enhancements are aimed at making the version look and feel more refined, especially for a game that is likely to stress current hardware heavily.

The broader implication is that even high-profile games are still being tuned around the practical limits of current console architectures. That means the highest-end visual experience may remain reserved for the premium version, while the standard consoles keep the game reachable and stable, even if it does not push the same frame-rate targets.
