### Quick Report

NVIDIA has added Preset C, a new option for DLSS Frame Generation that the company says can raise generation speed by as much as 35% on an RTX 5060. The setting is part of the latest NVIDIA App beta and is also supported on RTX 40-series, RTX 50-series, and RTX Spark hardware.
<!-- more -->

The updated model is intended to reduce the processing cost of generated frames, with NVIDIA reporting only minor image-quality trade-offs. It also applies to Multi-Frame Generation and Dynamic Multi-Frame Generation, giving users a new performance choice rather than a change that automatically affects every game.
