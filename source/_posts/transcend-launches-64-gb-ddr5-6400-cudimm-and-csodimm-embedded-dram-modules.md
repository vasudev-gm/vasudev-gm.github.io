### Quick Report

Transcend has launched DDR5-6400 CUDIMM and CSODIMM embedded DRAM modules with capacities of up to 64 GB per module. Designed for edge AI, smart manufacturing, industrial automation, and embedded systems, the modules operate at 1.1 V and include a Client Clock Driver to improve signal stability at high speeds.
<!-- more -->

The modules also integrate a power-management IC, a temperature sensor in the SPD chip, a transient-voltage suppressor, and on-die ECC. Those features are intended to improve voltage regulation, thermal monitoring, protection from fluctuations, and data integrity during long-running industrial workloads. Transcend says the products are developed and manufactured in Taiwan and backed by a limited lifetime warranty.
