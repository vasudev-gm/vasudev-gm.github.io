### Quick Report

TCL has demonstrated a 120 Hz IPS LCD prototype in a Lenovo Yoga laptop that reportedly draws about 1 W of power. The low-power panel could improve mobile battery life without abandoning the color and viewing-angle qualities associated with IPS displays.
<!-- more -->

The 1 W figure is likely for the panel under a controlled test condition, not the complete laptop or every brightness setting. TCL\'s prototype still needs commercial validation for uniformity, response time, cost, brightness, and manufacturing yield.

If the technology reaches retail systems, it could be especially useful for thin laptops that spend much of their time on office work or idle displays. The prototype shows that LCD efficiency remains a meaningful area of innovation alongside OLED.
