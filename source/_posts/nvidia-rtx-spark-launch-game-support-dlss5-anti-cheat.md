### Quick Report

NVIDIA says its RTX Spark systems will run dozens of games at launch, with DLSS 5 support and work underway to make anti-cheat compatible with competitive online titles. The company is targeting 1440p gaming and lists games such as Counter-Strike 2, Fortnite, and Rocket League among the supported lineup.
<!-- more -->

RTX Spark combines an Arm-based Grace processor with a Blackwell integrated GPU, so game compatibility depends on native versions or software translation as well as cooperation from anti-cheat providers. NVIDIA says a native RTX Spark edition of the 2027 Call of Duty release is also planned; the platform is scheduled to launch on October 16.
