### Quick Report

Jackery has introduced the HomePower 1000 Plus V2, a compact backup power station built around a 1,024 Wh baseline capacity and an 1,800 W continuous output. The unit is designed for home outages, remote work, and outdoor use, and it can expand to as much as 11 kWh with additional battery packs.
<!-- more -->

The design includes four pure sine-wave AC outlets, multiple USB-C and USB-A ports, and a fast-charging setup that can reach 80 percent in 36 minutes on AC power. The product also includes UPS-style switching in under 10 milliseconds, surge protection, and a 5-year warranty, while pricing starts at $749 with a launch-period sale down to $619.
