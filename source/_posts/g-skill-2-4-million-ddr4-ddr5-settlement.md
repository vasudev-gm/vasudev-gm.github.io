### Quick Report

G.Skill has agreed to pay a $2.4 million settlement to buyers of certain DDR4 and DDR5 memory kits. The settlement resolves a consumer dispute involving the affected products, though the exact eligibility and payment process will depend on the official terms.

Customers who believe they are included should review the settlement notice, deadlines, and documentation requirements rather than relying on informal summaries. The case also highlights the importance of clear product claims and specifications in the memory market.
