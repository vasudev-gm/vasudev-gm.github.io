### Quick Report

XMG has refreshed both the APEX 16 and PRO 16 VE with NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5070 12GB graphics and improved cooling designs. The new APEX 16 uses AMD Ryzen 9 9955HX or 8945HX silicon, while the PRO 16 VE keeps its Intel Core i9-14900HX platform but adds a stronger thermal layout and better ventilation.
<!-- more -->

The updated laptops aim to deliver more sustained performance under gaming and workstation loads without pushing temperatures and noise as aggressively as their earlier versions. XMG also keeps the same 16-inch 1600p 300 Hz IPS panels, 115 W GPU power targets, and user-upgradeable memory and storage options, while positioning the refreshed models as a cleaner value in the premium gaming laptop market.
