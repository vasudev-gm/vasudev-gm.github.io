### Quick Report

The latest No Man\'s Sky update adds AMD FSR 4 upscaling to the game. Version 7.15 also brings new holiday content, while Vulkan support in AMD FidelityFX SDK 2.4 makes the FSR 4 integration possible.
<!-- more -->

The game previously could not use FSR 4 through Vulkan, as the upscaler had been limited to DirectX 12. Developer Martin Griffiths says the SDK update enables the feature on Vulkan, with the potential for higher-quality upscaling and fewer artifacts while retaining performance benefits.
