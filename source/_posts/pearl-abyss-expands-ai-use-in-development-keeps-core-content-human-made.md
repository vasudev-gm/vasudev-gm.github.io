### Quick Report

Pearl Abyss plans to use AI more broadly across game development, while keeping core gameplay and the actual content of its games in human hands. The studio says its tools support tasks such as repetitive-work automation, early concept validation, and testing rather than replacing developers or generating finished game content.
<!-- more -->

The policy follows criticism after AI-generated assets appeared in Crimson Desert and were later removed; Pearl Abyss said those materials were placeholders that had mistakenly made it into the released game. Its latest position distinguishes using AI to speed up development workflows from using generated assets as part of the final experience.

That distinction reflects a wider industry debate about acceptable AI use in games. Studios may see clear value in faster prototyping and quality checks, while players remain sensitive to whether the final art and gameplay are created by the development team or generated by tools.
