### Quick Report

The Nintendo Switch emulator suyu has received version 0.0.4 with a recompiler mode that translates Switch AArch64 game code into native x86-64 executables on PC. The ahead-of-time approach reportedly delivers a major performance improvement over traditional hardware-level emulation while leaving both modes available to users.
<!-- more -->

The release is also effectively a final update, because the project\'s GitHub repository has been archived. Suyu follows earlier Switch emulator projects such as Yuzu and Ryujinx, which stopped development after legal pressure from Nintendo. By publishing the recompiler before archiving the project, the developers have left the open-source community with a more capable final release.
