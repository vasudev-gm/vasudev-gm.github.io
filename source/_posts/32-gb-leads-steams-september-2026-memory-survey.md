### Quick Report

In the September 2026 Steam survey, 32 GB systems reached 42.22% of participating PCs, moving ahead of 16 GB at 37.82%. That reverses the ranking from the prior survey, when 16 GB led by a small margin.
<!-- more -->

The result is a sharp one-month swing, not proof of a lasting shift: the survey is voluntary, and its sample changed substantially, including a jump in Simplified Chinese respondents. Steam reports installed capacity, but not memory speed or whether systems use DDR4 or DDR5; another survey will show whether the new lead holds.
