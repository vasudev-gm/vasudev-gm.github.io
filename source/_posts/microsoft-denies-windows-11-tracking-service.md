### Quick Report

Microsoft has denied reports of a new tracking service in Windows 11. The response addresses concerns that a recent or planned system component could expand monitoring of user activity.
<!-- more -->

Privacy claims around operating-system services can spread quickly when names, telemetry behavior, or policy changes are unclear. Users should rely on Microsoft documentation and observable settings rather than assuming that every reported service represents a new tracking feature.
