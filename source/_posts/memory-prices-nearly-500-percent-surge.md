### Quick Report

Memory prices have reportedly surged by nearly 500 percent over the last 12 months, setting a new record. The increase adds cost pressure across PCs, servers, smartphones, and other products that depend on DRAM and related memory components.
<!-- more -->

Such a sharp rise can affect retail module prices, system configurations, and manufacturers’ margins. The eventual market response will depend on inventory levels, production expansion, demand, and how quickly suppliers can restore balance.
