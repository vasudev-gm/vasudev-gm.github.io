### Quick Report

A new report says publishers could save more than $10 per copy by shifting AAA games to digital licensing instead of physical discs. According to the report, Sony and Microsoft may be paying roughly 15% to 20% of a game MSRP to cover disc manufacturing and distribution, a cost that can quickly add up for big-budget releases.
<!-- more -->

That makes disc-free distribution attractive for publishers, especially as storage needs and patch sizes keep rising. The same report points to games like Grand Theft Auto VI, where a $79.99 launch price could mean $12 to $16 in physical media costs per copy, and it also helps explain why console makers and publishers are pushing more aggressively toward digital-first models.
