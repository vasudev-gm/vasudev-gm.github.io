### Quick Report

Sennheiser has unveiled the Momentum True Wireless 5 earbuds with user-replaceable batteries. The design addresses a common limitation of compact wireless audio products, whose batteries can eventually determine the usable life of the device.
<!-- more -->

Replaceable batteries could improve repairability and reduce electronic waste, provided replacement parts and service procedures remain available. Buyers will also weigh sound quality, active noise cancellation, comfort, charging features, and the earbuds’ software support.
