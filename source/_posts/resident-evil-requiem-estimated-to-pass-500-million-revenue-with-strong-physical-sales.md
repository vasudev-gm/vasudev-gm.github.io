### Quick Report

An Alinea Analytics estimate gives Resident Evil Requiem a total of about 8.5 million copies sold across the current PlayStation, PC, and Xbox platforms. Its model also puts the game\'s combined revenue beyond $500 million, although these numbers are an external estimate rather than a financial statement from Capcom.
<!-- more -->

The estimated platform mix is 48.1% for PS5, 43.4% for Steam, and 8.6% for Xbox Series. The PS5 portion is also notable for its format split: approximately 30.8% is attributed to discs. That does not establish an official sales ledger, but it does suggest that a large contemporary release can still generate meaningful boxed-game demand alongside its digital business.
