### Quick Report

Pando is a new open-source split ergonomic mechanical keyboard designed to eliminate the usual pain points of custom DIY boards. It ships as a complete, integrated design rather than a solder-your-own kit, using an STM32 microcontroller and USB-C connection between the two halves.
<!-- more -->

The board is hot-swap compatible with MX switches and supports Vial-based remapping, macros, and layer customization. Its layout keeps a num row and offers a split design with thumb keys, aiming to make ergonomic keyboards easier for newcomers to adopt without forcing a highly niche build process or expensive custom parts. At $109, it sits in the middle ground between cheap hobby kits and premium split-keyboard products.
