### Quick Report

Bolt Graphics says its ZEUS test chip powered on and passed initial checks, while the company has opened a Taipei office to work more closely with manufacturing partners. The startup says the 6 mm², 12 nm test part completed bring-up in 24 hours and draws 1.4 W.
<!-- more -->

This silicon is a test vehicle, not the finished graphics card: Bolt plans to use TSMC N5P for the commercial ZEUS GPU. Developer access is targeted for early 2027, with limited customer availability later that year and volume production of cards and servers expected in 2028.
