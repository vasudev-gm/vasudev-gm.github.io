### Quick Report

Steam Deck LCD battery replacement parts have reportedly returned to iFixit, but at a steeply higher price. The availability is useful for owners who want to repair older handhelds, although the new cost may make replacement less attractive.
<!-- more -->

Repairable parts can extend the useful life of a handheld and reduce electronic waste. Owners should compare the replacement price with professional repair services and consider the difficulty of opening the device before attempting the work themselves.
