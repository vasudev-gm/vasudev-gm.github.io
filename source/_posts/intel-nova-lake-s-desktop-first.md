### Quick Report

Intel is deliberately bringing its newest CPU cores to desktop users before enterprise Xeon servers, with Robert Hallock saying the company wants enthusiasts to evaluate the design first. The comment lines up with the upcoming Nova Lake-S generation, where the new Coyote Cove P-cores and Arctic Wolf E-cores are expected to appear on desktop hardware before the same architecture reaches server platforms.
