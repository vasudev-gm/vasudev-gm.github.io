### Quick Report

Microsoft is unifying its Copilot features into a single super app. The change is intended to give users one place to access the company’s growing collection of AI capabilities instead of navigating separate Copilot experiences.
<!-- more -->

Consolidation could make Copilot easier to discover and maintain a more consistent user experience across Microsoft products. It also places more features behind one application, making clear navigation, privacy controls, and reliable integration important as the service expands.
