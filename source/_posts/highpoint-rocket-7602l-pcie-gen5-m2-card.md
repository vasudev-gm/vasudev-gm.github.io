### Quick Report

HighPoint has launched the Rocket 7602L, a bootable dual-port PCIe Gen 5 M.2 add-in card. The adapter is designed to add two high-speed NVMe storage connections to compatible systems.
<!-- more -->

Boot support allows the card to host an operating-system drive while the second port can serve additional storage needs. Performance and compatibility will depend on motherboard firmware, PCIe lane allocation, cooling, and the NVMe drives installed.
