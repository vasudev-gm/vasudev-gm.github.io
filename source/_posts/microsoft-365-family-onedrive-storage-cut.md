### Quick Report

Microsoft 365 Family plans appear to be reducing the total OneDrive storage pool from 6 TB to 2 TB. The change would affect how households manage shared files and makes the storage cap more restrictive at a time when many families are working, storing, and syncing larger media libraries across multiple devices.
<!-- more -->

The reduction is significant because Microsoft 365 Family plans usually present storage as a shared pool across the household, which can be convenient for backup and collaboration. A drop from 6 TB to 2 TB would compress that headroom quickly for photo libraries, device backups, and large work archives, forcing users to rethink how they allocate space between members.

For Microsoft, the adjustment may be a pricing or policy recalibration rather than a purely technical change. For customers, the practical effect is a sharper limit on what a single family plan can hold, especially as personal backups, video files, and cross-device sync habits keep growing.
