### Quick Report

The European Union is pressuring Apple to provide a universal clipboard for better compatibility with Windows. The proposal would make it easier to copy and paste content across Apple devices and Windows systems.
<!-- more -->

Cross-platform clipboard support could reduce friction for users who work across ecosystems and currently depend on separate tools or services. The practical outcome will depend on the technical requirements, privacy safeguards, and how Apple implements any interoperability obligation.
