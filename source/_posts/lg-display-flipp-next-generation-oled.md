### Quick Report

LG Display has unveiled FLIPP, a next-generation OLED technology. The platform is intended to advance OLED display capabilities and could support new designs across consumer electronics and other display products.
<!-- more -->

The technology’s importance will depend on measurable improvements in brightness, efficiency, durability, manufacturing cost, and form-factor flexibility. Commercial products and availability will determine how quickly FLIPP reaches consumers.
