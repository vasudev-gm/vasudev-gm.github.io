### Quick Report

Kioxia and SanDisk have unveiled 10th-generation 332-layer QLC NAND with interface speeds reaching 4.8 GB per second. The new flash generation targets higher density and faster data movement for modern storage products.
<!-- more -->

More layers can increase storage density while QLC technology helps lower cost per bit, although endurance and sustained performance remain important trade-offs. The interface improvements could support faster SSD designs once controllers and products using the NAND become available.
