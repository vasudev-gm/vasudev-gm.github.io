AVerMedia has unveiled high-performance capture solutions for next-generation livestreaming. The products target streamers and creators who need to capture, process, and deliver high-quality video while keeping latency and system overhead under control.

Modern capture hardware must balance resolution, refresh rate, encoding support, connectivity, and software compatibility. The products’ practical value will depend on their supported formats, passthrough capabilities, pricing, and performance in real streaming setups.
