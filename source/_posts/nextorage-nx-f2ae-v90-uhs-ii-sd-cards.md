### Quick Report

Nextorage has released NX-F2AE V90 SDXC UHS-II memory cards. The cards target cameras and other devices that need high sustained write performance for demanding photo and video recording.
<!-- more -->

The V90 classification is aimed at workloads requiring consistent write speeds, including high-bitrate video. Buyers will still need to confirm capacity, reader compatibility, endurance, and the actual recording modes supported by their camera.
