### Quick Report

LG has announced U.S. availability and pricing for the UltraGear 25G590B, a 25-inch Full HD gaming monitor with a native 1000 Hz refresh rate. The display costs $999.99 and is aimed at competitive players who prioritize motion clarity and input response in fast-paced games.
<!-- more -->

The IPS monitor combines 1,920 x 1,080 resolution with Dynamic Action Sync, Motion Blur Reduction Pro, NVIDIA G-SYNC compatibility, and AMD FreeSync Premium. It also includes an FPS counter, low-reflection film, adjustable stand, and on-device AI Scene Optimization and AI Sound features; pre-orders through August 31 include two years of LG Premium Care for $1.
