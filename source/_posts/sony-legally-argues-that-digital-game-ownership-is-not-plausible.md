### Quick Report

Sony has argued in a California class-action case that consumers do not realistically own digital games, because the purchase is governed by a software license rather than a title transfer. The company says the terms clearly state that software is licensed rather than sold, and that buyers therefore cannot reasonably expect ownership status.
<!-- more -->

The legal dispute is part of a broader debate about digital rights, licensing, and consumer expectations in gaming. Sony\'s position emphasizes that the business model of digital storefronts depends on license-based access rather than permanent ownership, which could shape future legal and policy decisions around digital game purchases.
