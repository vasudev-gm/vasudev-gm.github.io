An NVIDIA RTX Spark laptop was reportedly found on the roadside and reviewed before the platform\'s official launch. The unusual early look offers a preview of NVIDIA\'s upcoming laptop hardware and its approach to bringing RTX-class AI capabilities to portable systems.
