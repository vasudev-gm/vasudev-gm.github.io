### Quick Report

US sales for Xbox and PlayStation appear to be cooling as average console prices remain near record levels, a combination that suggests the market is becoming less forgiving for higher-cost hardware. The pressure is not coming from one product alone; it reflects a broader consumer environment in which buyers are weighing the cost of new hardware against the price of games, subscriptions, and accessories.
<!-- more -->

That sales trend matters because it highlights a structural challenge for both console makers: the premium hardware market can only absorb so much price growth before demand begins to weaken. Even if both companies continue to push premium features and ecosystem upgrades, the reality is that affordability is increasingly shaping purchase decisions in the US market.

The result is a harder path for console makers hoping to grow volume without more aggressive discounts, promotions, or lower-cost hardware tiers. With average selling prices still elevated, the industry may be approaching a point where hardware revenue rises even as unit sales slow, which can mask a more difficult underlying demand picture.
