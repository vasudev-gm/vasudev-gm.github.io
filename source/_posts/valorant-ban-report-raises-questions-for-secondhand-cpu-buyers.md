### Quick Report

The buyer of a secondhand Ryzen 7 5800X3D says Valorant blocked the game at launch and Riot support attributed the restriction to cheating by the person who previously owned the processor. The Riot anti-cheat lead disputed claims that the motherboard and storage were also banned, saying hardware bans last up to four months and apply to the affected game.
<!-- more -->

Riot has not explained how Vanguard identifies the processor, and a CPU does not expose a unique serial number to Windows. A firmware TPM is one possibility, not a confirmed mechanism; the reported ban should therefore not be treated as proof that a particular component was uniquely identified.
