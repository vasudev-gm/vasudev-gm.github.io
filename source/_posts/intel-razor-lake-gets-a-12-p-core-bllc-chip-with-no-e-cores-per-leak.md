### Quick Report

A fresh leak suggests Intel may be preparing a Razor Lake processor with a 12-core performance-focused design and no E-cores, a notable departure from the hybrid-core approach that has defined much of the company’s recent roadmap. The design reportedly includes a bLLC cache configuration, which would point to a more aggressive single-thread and cache-sensitive layout.
<!-- more -->

The underlying idea appears to be a more homogeneous CPU profile that prioritizes raw core throughput and cache performance instead of mixing many smaller efficiency cores. That would fit Intel’s broader effort to optimize for workloads where headroom and latency matter more than thread-count efficiency.

If the leak proves accurate, it would also signal that Intel is still exploring different core mixes to balance laptop and desktop performance in a difficult market. The bigger question is whether this is a niche high-end product or a preview of how the company wants to position part of the next generation.
