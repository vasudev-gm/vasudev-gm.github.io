### Quick Report

TCL is reportedly preparing OLED panels that target 700 Hz refresh rates by 2028, which sounds extreme on paper but also explains why display makers are still chasing ever-higher motion clarity metrics. The real story is not simply about bigger numbers, but about the way panel vendors continue redefining what counts as a useful gaming display in a market where refresh-rate bragging rights are now a major product lever.
<!-- more -->

The challenge is that the jump from 240 Hz or 480 Hz to 700 Hz does not automatically create a visibly better experience for every user, especially on OLED displays where motion blur is already less severe than on LCDs. Even so, the work points to a future where premium gaming monitors increasingly compete on subtler improvements in response time, sample-and-hold behavior, and motion handling rather than raw pixel density alone. For consumers, the real question is whether those gains are meaningful enough to justify another round of expensive display hardware.
