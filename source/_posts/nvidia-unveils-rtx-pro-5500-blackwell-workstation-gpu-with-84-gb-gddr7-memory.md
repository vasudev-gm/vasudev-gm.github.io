### Quick Report

NVIDIA has added the RTX PRO 5500 Blackwell to its professional visualization lineup, pairing a GB202 GPU with 21,760 CUDA cores and 84 GB of GDDR7 memory. The memory uses a 416-bit bus and delivers approximately 1,398 GB/s of bandwidth, while 28 memory modules are arranged across both sides of the PCB.
<!-- more -->

The card sits below the RTX PRO 6000 but far above the RTX PRO 5000 in memory capacity and compute resources. NVIDIA rates it at a 600 W TDP and offers air-cooled and liquid-cooled designs for workstation and rack deployments. Multi-instance GPU support can split the 84 GB pool into two 42 GB instances, although pricing and availability have not yet been announced.
