### Quick Report

NVIDIA is adding a 64 GB unified-memory option to DGX Spark through Acer, ASUS, Dell, Gigabyte, HP, and MSI, with availability scheduled for October 23 at $4,999. The system retains the GB10 Grace Blackwell chip and software stack used by the 128 GB configuration.
<!-- more -->

NVIDIA says a single system can run models with up to 100 billion parameters; two linked units can pool 128 GB and target models up to 200 billion parameters. These are vendor capabilities, not independent performance results, and the entry configuration halves the memory of the original model without changing its core hardware.
