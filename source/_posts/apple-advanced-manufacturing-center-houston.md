### Quick Report

Apple has opened an advanced manufacturing center in Houston, expanding its investment in manufacturing capabilities in the United States. The facility is expected to support new production methods and strengthen Apple’s relationship with domestic manufacturing partners.
<!-- more -->

The center could help Apple test and scale manufacturing technologies closer to its U.S. operations and customers. Its long-term impact will depend on the products, processes, and supplier activity that eventually use the facility.
