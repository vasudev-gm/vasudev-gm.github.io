### Quick Report

A newly published jailbreak called Relapse reportedly covers PlayStation 5 and PS5 Pro systems running firmware versions from 7.00 through 13.60. That gives the project unusually broad reach across Sony\'s current console family, although systems updated beyond the supported range may no longer be eligible.

The exploit can open the door to homebrew, emulators, and other unofficial software, but it also creates a choice between preserving access and staying current. Newer system updates can close the vulnerability, while older firmware may lose compatibility with future games, patches, and PlayStation Network features. Marvel\'s Wolverine is one reason the timing has drawn attention, since versions of the game and its updates target firmware in the same general range. The project is community-developed, so users should verify compatibility and understand the security and online-service risks before modifying a console.
