### Quick Report

International unions representing game workers have rallied against the increasing use of AI in video game development. Their joint statement argues that games are best made by people, not algorithms, and calls for industry leaders to prioritize human creativity and fair labor practices.
<!-- more -->

The unions warn that unchecked AI adoption could undermine jobs, working conditions, and the quality of creative output. They urge studios and publishers to engage with labor representatives when considering new technologies in the development process.
