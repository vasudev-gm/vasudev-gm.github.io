### Quick Report

Sonnet has launched a Thunderbolt 5 external GPU box with an 850 W power supply and support for triple-slot graphics cards. The enclosure is designed to give laptops and compact systems access to desktop-class graphics hardware without requiring an internal GPU upgrade.
<!-- more -->

The combination of a high-power supply and triple-slot clearance makes the enclosure more practical for large modern GPUs, which are often too demanding for smaller eGPU docks. Thunderbolt 5 bandwidth still remains a key consideration because external graphics performance depends on the connection as well as the card itself, but the design gives enthusiasts more flexibility when building portable or modular systems.
