### Quick Report

Frore Systems has announced LiquidJet diamond cold plates for AI factories, a move aimed at the thermal bottlenecks created by high-density compute clusters and accelerated AI workloads. The company is pitching the design as a way to spread heat more effectively while keeping cooling systems compact enough for dense server environments.
<!-- more -->

Diamond has excellent thermal conductivity, which makes it attractive for removing heat from power-hungry chips that can otherwise sit near their thermal limits. In a data center context, that matters because AI accelerators and CPUs are increasingly packed into racks where airflow, pumping efficiency, and surface contact all shape performance and reliability.

The bigger implication is that cooling is becoming a strategic part of AI infrastructure design, not just a support function. As server density rises and power draw climbs, the most effective systems will be the ones that keep chips stable without forcing expensive redesigns of the entire rack or facility.
