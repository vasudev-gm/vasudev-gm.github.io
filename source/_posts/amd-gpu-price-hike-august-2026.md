### Quick Report

AMD is reportedly planning to raise GPU prices by 10 percent in August. If implemented, the increase could affect Radeon graphics cards and add pressure to an already competitive market for gaming and workstation hardware.
<!-- more -->

Higher prices may push buyers toward discounted inventory, competing products, or used hardware. Retail pricing will still depend on board partners, local taxes, supply, and promotions, so customers should compare multiple stores before making a purchase.
