### Quick Report

ASRock is bringing its Taichi brand to the monitor market with panels offering refresh rates of up to 720 Hz. The expansion takes the company beyond PC components and targets competitive gamers who prioritize motion clarity and extremely low display latency.
<!-- more -->

A 720 Hz panel is aimed at a narrow audience because reaching those refresh rates requires a capable graphics pipeline and games that can sustain very high frame rates. The broader Taichi lineup will be judged on more than its headline refresh rate, including resolution, response behavior, connectivity, adaptive sync, and image quality. Even so, the launch shows how aggressively monitor makers are competing for esports-focused buyers.
