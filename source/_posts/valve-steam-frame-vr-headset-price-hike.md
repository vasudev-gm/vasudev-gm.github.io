### Quick Report

Valve\'s upcoming Steam Frame VR headset may face a price increase before launch. Reports indicate that Qualcomm, the headset\'s main chip supplier, is raising processor prices by double-digit percentages, adding pressure to the hardware\'s expected cost.
<!-- more -->

The reported increase comes alongside broader memory, storage, and component cost pressure across the hardware market. Valve has not established a final consumer price in the report, leaving the launch price and how much of the added cost reaches buyers unresolved.
