### Quick Report

Microsoft says Advanced Shader Delivery will support GeForce RTX cards and the RTX Spark platform later this month. The system lets developers package hardware-specific, precompiled shaders with a game, reducing the amount players need to compile locally.
<!-- more -->

This is a compatibility expansion, not a new GPU feature or a guaranteed fix for every title. Microsoft has also listed Intel Arc B-series and newer Core Ultra graphics among the platforms gaining support in the same rollout, broadening the hardware covered by the delivery system.
