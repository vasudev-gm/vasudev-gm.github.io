### Quick Report

AMD is preparing a broad pricing reset as TSMC raises wafer costs and the supply chain remains under pressure. According to reports, the company may implement a 10% increase across GPUs, chipset products, and possibly some CPU lines, which would ripple into prices for partners and end users alike.
<!-- more -->

The company has raised prices before, but this would be notable because it could also affect motherboard chipsets and potentially some desktop and mobile CPU segments. For a fabless company like AMD, these higher costs cannot be absorbed easily, so the likely outcome is a pass-through to the market as customers face a more expensive hardware stack.
