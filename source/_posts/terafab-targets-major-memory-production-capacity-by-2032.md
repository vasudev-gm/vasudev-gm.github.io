### Quick Report

Terafab wants to become one of the world\'s largest memory makers by 2032, with reports outlining a target of roughly 700,000 memory wafers per month. The figure is part of a broader proposed capacity of about one million wafers monthly.
<!-- more -->

If achieved, the memory portion alone would approach Samsung\'s current total DRAM wafer capacity, underscoring the scale of the ambition. These are long-range targets, not present-day production figures; the plan still depends on building and ramping the required manufacturing capacity.
