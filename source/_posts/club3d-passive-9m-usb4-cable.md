### Quick Report

Club3D has launched a fully passive USB4 cable measuring 9 meters in length. The cable is designed to provide long-distance USB4 connectivity without relying on active signal electronics.
<!-- more -->

A passive cable at this length could simplify connections between PCs, docks, displays, and other peripherals. Actual performance will depend on the supported USB4 mode, power delivery requirements, device compatibility, and signal conditions.
