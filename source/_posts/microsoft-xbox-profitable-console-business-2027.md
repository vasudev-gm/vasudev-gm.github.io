### Quick Report

Microsoft is reportedly pressuring Xbox to make its console business more profitable by 2027. The goal could influence hardware strategy, subscription pricing, game distribution, and the balance between console sales and broader gaming services.
<!-- more -->

Xbox will need to improve margins without weakening the value proposition that attracts players to its ecosystem. Any changes may affect future hardware plans, first-party releases, and how Microsoft positions Xbox across consoles, PCs, and cloud gaming.
