### Quick Report

Samsung continues to lead the global DRAM market, while Micron is reportedly gaining ground on the memory giant. The shifting market shares highlight the continuing competition among the major suppliers as demand for server, AI, and consumer memory evolves.
<!-- more -->

Samsung’s lead gives it significant scale across conventional DRAM and high-bandwidth memory products, but Micron’s progress could make the supplier landscape more competitive. Future results will depend on production capacity, pricing, technology transitions, and demand from data-center customers.
