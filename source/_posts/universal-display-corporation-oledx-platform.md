### Quick Report

Universal Display Corporation has announced its OLEDX platform to improve OLED efficiency. The technology targets one of the display industry’s key challenges: delivering strong image quality while reducing power consumption.
<!-- more -->

More efficient OLED materials and structures could benefit smartphones, laptops, televisions, and other battery-powered displays. Commercial impact will depend on panel integration, manufacturing yields, lifetime, and adoption by display makers.
