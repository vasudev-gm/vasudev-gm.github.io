### Quick Report

Patches for AMD RDNA 5 support in the DCN6 display engine are arriving in the Linux kernel. The work adds early software groundwork for display functionality on future AMD graphics hardware.
<!-- more -->

Kernel enablement ahead of hardware availability helps the open-source graphics stack mature before products reach users. More driver work will still be needed for complete display features, validation, and stable support across Linux distributions.
