### Quick Report

Microsoft is reportedly increasing Windows licensing fees for OEMs by 7 to 10 percent, a move that could make pre-built laptops and desktops more expensive. The increase is expected to affect PC makers that bundle Windows with new systems, even though retail licensing prices for individual customers remain unchanged.
<!-- more -->

The change is another reminder that the cost of a Windows PC is shaped by more than just hardware components. As OEMs absorb higher licensing costs, buyers may see modest price increases on mainstream systems, especially in markets where margins are already tight.
