### Quick Report

KDE is drafting explicit guidance on AI use that pushes contributors to stay human in the loop rather than treating tools as a shortcut around judgment. The draft is blunt in places, including the memorable warning to avoid becoming a "meat proxy," a phrase aimed at submissions that look AI-generated but do not reflect real understanding of the code.
<!-- more -->

The proposal is less about banning AI outright and more about preserving quality and maintainability. KDE seems to want contributors to use tools as helpers, not as a substitute for reasoning, review, or the work of learning a codebase. That approach lines up with a broader open-source concern: when AI-generated patches are low quality or unreviewed, the burden falls on maintainers and the health of the project suffers.
