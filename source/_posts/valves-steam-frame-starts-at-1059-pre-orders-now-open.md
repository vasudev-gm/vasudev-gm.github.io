### Quick Report

Valve has opened reservations for the Steam Frame, with the 256 GB model starting at $1,059 and the 1 TB version priced at $1,299. Sign-ups close on September 17, and Valve expects to send the first purchase invitations on September 18 through a randomized reservation lottery.
<!-- more -->

The headset\'s accessories include a $29 power supply, a $59 Ergonomic Accessories Kit, and a $49 replacement kit for the face gasket and cushions. The pricing is higher than Valve\'s earlier target of staying below the $999 Index, and the company has pointed to elevated DRAM and storage costs as a factor affecting hardware prices across its product lineup.
