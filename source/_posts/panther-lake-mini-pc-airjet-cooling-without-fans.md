### Quick Report

A mini PC based on Intel Panther Lake uses Frore Systems' AirJet solid-state cooling instead of conventional fans, with reported noise below 21 dBA. The design aims to pair a compact form factor with cooling that avoids the familiar fan noise of small, high-performance computers.

AirJet uses vibrating membranes to move air through a system, replacing the bulkier fan assembly with a solid-state approach. In a tightly packed mini PC, that can help manufacturers manage both acoustic output and thermal constraints, although sustained performance will ultimately depend on the device\'s power limits and chassis design.

The system is an example of how alternative cooling methods may enable quieter compact PCs without making them passively cooled in the traditional heatsink-only sense. Independent temperature and performance testing will show how well this approach handles prolonged workloads.
