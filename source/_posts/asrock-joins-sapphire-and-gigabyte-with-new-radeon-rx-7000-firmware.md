### Quick Report

ASRock has published new vBIOS updates for multiple Radeon RX 7000 series cards, joining Sapphire and GIGABYTE in rolling out fresh firmware for the current generation of AMD graphics hardware. The company quietly posted version 1.0.5 updates on October 29, covering a range of RX 7600 XT, RX 7700 XT, RX 7800 XT, and RX 7900 series models.
<!-- more -->

The details remain sparse because ASRock did not list the exact fix in the release notes, but the timing strongly suggests a related security patch connected to AMD vulnerability reports that started surfacing in early October. GIGABYTE later tied its own files to the AMD-SN-6023 identifier, suggesting a pattern across vendors rather than an isolated firmware problem.

The broader issue is that even as AMD has not yet published a full public advisory, board partners are moving quickly to roll out patches. That indicates a security concern worth taking seriously, even if the precise impact is still being evaluated behind the scenes by the ecosystem.
