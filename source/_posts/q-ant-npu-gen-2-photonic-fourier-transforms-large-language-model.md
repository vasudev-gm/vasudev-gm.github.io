### Quick Report

Q.ANT has unveiled NPU Gen 2+, a photonic processor architecture that uses optical Fourier transformations to accelerate parts of large-language-model workloads. The design combines optical processing with conventional electronics to target expensive mathematical operations in AI inference.
<!-- more -->

The Fouriers-based approach is attractive because the transform can be performed directly in the optical domain, potentially reducing the number of digital operations and associated memory movement. The company is presenting the platform as a way to improve efficiency for model workloads without relying only on conventional GPU and accelerator scaling.

Real-world impact will depend on integration, software support, and whether the system can deliver the claimed gains outside a controlled demonstration. Photonic AI is still an emerging field, and practical benefits must be proven across full model pipelines rather than isolated mathematical kernels.
