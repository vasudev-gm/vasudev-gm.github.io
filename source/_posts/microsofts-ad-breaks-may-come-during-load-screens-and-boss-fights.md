### Quick Report

A Microsoft patent describes ad breaks appearing during loading screens and after boss fights, outlining a more aggressive monetization approach for games. The concept would turn previously neutral transition moments into advertising opportunities.
<!-- more -->

The idea is notable because it reflects a broader push to find new revenue streams inside games without relying only on premium pricing or microtransactions. In practice, it would make the user experience feel more commercially sponsored at exactly the moments when players are already waiting or wrapping up a challenge.

The larger concern is not just the patent itself but the precedent it sets: companies are increasingly exploring ways to insert promotional content into core gameplay moments, even when the player is focused on immersion. It is a reminder that advertising may become a much more visible part of the game experience if the business case continues to improve.
