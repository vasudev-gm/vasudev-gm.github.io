### Quick Report

A teardown of a PS5 Slim from the Wolverine bundle shows a revised board labeled EDM-051. The most notable thermal change is a new barrier around the processor that helps contain its liquid-metal interface material, alongside a reworked heatsink.
<!-- more -->

The same teardown identifies a lower-cost HDMI retimer and a CMOS battery moved to a more accessible position. Sony has not confirmed the revision, and no independent tests establish whether it runs cooler or lasts longer; it is also unknown how widely this board version will ship.
