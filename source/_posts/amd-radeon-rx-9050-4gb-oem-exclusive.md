### Quick Report

AMD\'s Radeon RX 9050 4 GB graphics card will reportedly be exclusive to OEM systems rather than receiving a normal retail launch. The entry-level RDNA 4 model has drawn attention because 4 GB of VRAM is increasingly restrictive for modern games and graphics workloads.
<!-- more -->

Keeping the 4 GB card inside prebuilt systems gives AMD and its partners a lower-cost configuration, but buyers will need to check the exact memory capacity before purchasing. A separate 8 GB version is expected to be the more relevant option for retail customers.
