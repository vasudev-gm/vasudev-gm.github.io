### Quick Report

CD Projekt Red says The Witcher 4 is already running on all of its target platforms: PC, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X|S. The statement reflects a major lesson learned from Cyberpunk 2077, when the studio pushed for high-end PC performance first and then struggled to scale the game down to console hardware.
<!-- more -->

The co-CEO explained that the team now wants to validate the game on every target system well before launch, rather than assuming that the process will be easy. With a 2028 release window already in place, the practical effect is that the project is much farther along in platform validation than the studio was during the Cyberpunk 2077 development cycle.
