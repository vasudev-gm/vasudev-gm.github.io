### Quick Report

Apple has promoted John Ternus to CEO, handing leadership of the company to a long-time hardware executive who was closely associated with the Mac transition to Apple Silicon. Ternus has spent years leading hardware engineering and product development across the iPhone, iPad, Mac, AirPods, and Vision Pro lines.
<!-- more -->

The appointment is notable because Ternus is seen as the deeply technical choice inside Apple, the leader who helped steer the company from Intel-based Macs to custom in-house silicon. In the broader market, that signals continuity around Apple\'s strategy of tightly integrating hardware, software, and silicon design.
