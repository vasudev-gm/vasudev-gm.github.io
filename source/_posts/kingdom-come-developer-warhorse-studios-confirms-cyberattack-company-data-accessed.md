### Quick Report

Warhorse Studios, the developer behind Kingdom Come: Deliverance, has confirmed a cyberattack and said company data was accessed. The incident underscores how even well-established game studios are exposed to increasingly common security threats that target internal systems and sensitive operational information.
<!-- more -->

The impact of such breaches can be much broader than a single game launch or product delay, because studios often rely on internal design files, employee records, and partner information that are all valuable to attackers. For gamers and developers alike, incidents like this are a reminder that security is now part of the operational backbone of modern game creation.
