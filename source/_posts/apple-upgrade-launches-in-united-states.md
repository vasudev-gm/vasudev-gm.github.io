### Quick Report

Apple has launched Apple Upgrade in the United States as a leasing program provided by Klarna. The program covers iPhone, Apple Watch, Mac, and iPad purchases through Apple\'s online store, app, and retail locations.
<!-- more -->

The plans use different lease durations depending on the product, giving customers another way to spread payments instead of buying outright or using traditional financing. Apple Upgrade also marks a shift in the company\'s upgrade and payment options for customers in the US market.
