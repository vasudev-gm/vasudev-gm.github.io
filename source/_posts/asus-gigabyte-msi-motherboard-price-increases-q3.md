### Quick Report

ASUS, Gigabyte, and MSI are reportedly preparing to raise motherboard prices in the third quarter. The move could increase the cost of new PC builds and upgrades across several market segments.
<!-- more -->

Price changes may vary by region, chipset, and product tier rather than affecting every board equally. Buyers planning a build may compare current inventory with upcoming models, while manufacturers will need to balance component costs against demand.
