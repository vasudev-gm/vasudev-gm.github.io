### Quick Report

Intel has appointed Dean Jarnac as executive vice president and chief sales officer. He will lead the company global sales organization across client, data center, AI, networking, and ASIC products, reporting directly to CEO Lip-Bu Tan and joining the company in September.
<!-- more -->

Jarnac joins Intel from Marvell, where he served as chief sales officer and oversaw worldwide sales, field application engineering, and sales operations. He previously held senior sales roles at Broadcom and AMD and brings more than 30 years of semiconductor industry experience. Greg Ernst will leave Intel after 27 years as part of the transition, with the company framing the appointment as a step toward stronger customer relationships and improved go-to-market execution.
