### Quick Report

Microsoft is investigating reports that the Windows 11 August update is breaking games for some users. The issue adds to the risks of installing major operating-system updates immediately on gaming systems that depend on specific drivers and software combinations.
<!-- more -->

Affected players should monitor Microsoft’s support guidance and avoid removing system updates without understanding the security and compatibility consequences. A fix or workaround may vary by game, Windows build, graphics driver, and hardware configuration.
