### Quick Report

Seasonic is preparing its first consumer power supply to receive 80 PLUS Ruby certification. The high-efficiency design targets PC builders who want to reduce power loss while supporting demanding modern hardware.
<!-- more -->

Higher efficiency can reduce wasted energy and heat, although the purchase decision will also depend on capacity, noise, connectors, protections, and pricing. Certification results should be considered alongside independent testing under realistic loads.
