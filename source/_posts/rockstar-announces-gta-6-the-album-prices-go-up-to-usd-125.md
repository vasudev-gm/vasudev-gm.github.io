### Quick Report

Rockstar Games has announced Grand Theft Auto 6: The Album, a 34-track release tied to Atlantic Records and set to ship alongside the game on November 19. Six songs are already streaming on Spotify and Apple Music, while physical editions include a CD, a standard vinyl set, and a premium collectible with a liquid-filled disc priced at $124.98.
<!-- more -->

The premium vinyl option costs more than the $100 Ultimate Edition of GTA 6 itself, which makes the package feel more like a collector item than a typical soundtrack tie-in. The move also highlights how much value Rockstar is putting on the launch window, even as the market continues to push prices higher across premium game and media bundles.
