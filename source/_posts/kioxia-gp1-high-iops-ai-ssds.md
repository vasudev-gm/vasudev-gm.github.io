### Quick Report

Kioxia has announced the GP1 series of super-high-IOPS SSDs for artificial intelligence applications. The drives target storage workloads that require frequent, parallel access to large volumes of data.
<!-- more -->

High input-output performance can help keep accelerator systems supplied with data, although total platform performance also depends on controllers, networking, software, and the storage architecture. Enterprise buyers will additionally weigh endurance, capacity, power use, and availability.
