### Quick Report

Electronic Arts is adding 25 classic titles to Steam, and the line-up includes Road Rash, a long-running sign that old racing and action games are still finding new life through modern digital storefronts. The move is part of a broader effort to repack older content for players who want a simpler, more convenient way to revisit legacy catalog titles.
<!-- more -->

The significance is not just that Road Rash is back in digital form, but that publishers are increasingly treating older franchises as a steady source of value rather than as relics of the past. With older games now available through storefronts that can patch, save, and support modern systems, the catalog becomes easier to rediscover and easier to sell again.

For retro fans, the move is welcome because it broadens access without requiring original hardware or fragile media. For the industry, it reflects a familiar truth: legacy games still have commercial value, especially when they can be reintroduced with modern convenience and a lower cost to maintain.
