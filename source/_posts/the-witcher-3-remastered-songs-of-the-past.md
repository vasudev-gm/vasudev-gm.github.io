### Quick Report

CD Projekt RED has announced a remastered version of The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt with upgrades to visuals, performance, combat, traversal, monster behavior, skills, mounts, and photo mode. The remaster will launch on PC, PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, and Nintendo Switch 2, with current owners receiving the upgrade at no additional cost.
<!-- more -->

The studio also offered a first look at Songs of the Past, a paid expansion arriving in 2027 and introducing the new region of Letten, its folklore, festivals, and a hidden threat for Geralt to confront. Existing players also receive Hearts of Stone and Blood and Wine for free, while the remaster and expansion are planned for Battle.net alongside the established platforms.
