### Quick Report

Vince Lucido, known as Kingpin, says he is returning to hardware overclocking after finding a new partner in ASUS. In a video update, he mentioned having hardware from ASUS and Corsair and described discussions with ASUS management, but did not identify a product or launch date.
<!-- more -->

It is not yet clear whether the work will result in an ASUS-branded Kingpin graphics card; previous Lucido collaborations included EVGA and a planned PNY project that was later dropped. For now, the announcement confirms a partnership, while the hardware scope remains undisclosed.
