### Quick Report

Apple is reportedly developing gaming controllers under its Beats brand, with two products identified by the codenames T6502 and T1057. The controllers are expected to target iPhone gaming first and include familiar controls such as thumbsticks, triggers, shoulder buttons, and home controls.
<!-- more -->

References in the macOS 26.7 release candidate suggest that the T1057 includes an accelerometer and gyroscope, while the T6502 does not. That difference could point to separate designs, including one that uses an iPhone\'s sensors and another that can work more independently with third-party phones, handhelds, consoles, or PCs. Apple has not announced pricing or a release date.
