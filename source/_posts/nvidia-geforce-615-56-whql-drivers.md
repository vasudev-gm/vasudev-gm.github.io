### Quick Report

NVIDIA has released GeForce 615.56 WHQL Game Ready drivers with launch-day optimizations for STAR WARS Zero Company, Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy, and Hell Let Loose: Vietnam. The update also addresses frame-rate issues in DOOM: The Dark Ages and Indiana Jones and the Great Circle when DLSS Frame Generation 3x or higher is enabled.
<!-- more -->

The release fixes texture flickering in The Outer Worlds on R595 and R610 drivers, ASUS Armory Crate GameVisual profile problems, a GeForce RTX 4000 driver crash in Chief Architect, and incorrect 3D application detection in Claude Desktop. NVIDIA also resolved crashes in some 32-bit DirectX 12 applications and a 2 GB OpenCL buffer limitation in Tycho Tracker, while the Prefer Maximum Performance power setting remains a known issue.
