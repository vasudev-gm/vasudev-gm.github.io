### Quick Report

Rapidus has unveiled its CORE framework, a broader ecosystem plan designed to connect design partners, EDA tools, IP providers, and manufacturing players around advanced semiconductor development. The effort is meant to make it easier for customers to move from concept to mass production without coordinating every stage in isolation.
<!-- more -->

The framework is especially relevant because advanced semiconductor work is no longer just about a fab line. Design, verification, IP reuse, packaging coordination, and manufacturing readiness all shape whether a chip can be produced at scale. Rapidus is trying to solve that problem by creating a more complete support network while also addressing the talent shortage that has made global collaboration more important.

The first visible pillar is a Design Solution Associates group bringing together companies across the semiconductor value chain. That gives Rapidus a clearer route to support both Japanese and overseas customers while building the broader ecosystem needed for next-generation logic and memory development.
