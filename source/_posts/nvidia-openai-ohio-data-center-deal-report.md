### Quick Report

NVIDIA and OpenAI are reportedly discussing a deal valued at up to $250 billion in connection with a 10-gigawatt data center project in Ohio. The reported scale would make the proposal one of the largest infrastructure discussions linked to the rapidly expanding AI compute market.
<!-- more -->

The discussions remain unconfirmed, and a project of this size would require major power, networking, financing, and construction commitments. Even without a finalized agreement, the report shows how AI model demand is driving increasingly large partnerships between chip suppliers and compute providers.
