### Quick Report

ASUS is predicting that 8 GB of RAM could return as the standard specification for budget PCs. The forecast reflects pressure on low-cost systems as manufacturers balance component prices, practical performance, and the needs of mainstream software.
<!-- more -->

A lower-memory baseline may reduce the cost of entry-level desktops and laptops, but it could also make new PCs more sensitive to multitasking and modern application workloads. For buyers, the key question is whether 8 GB systems remain upgradeable and whether the saved cost is worth the likely need for a memory expansion later.

The prediction does not apply equally to every category. Gaming PCs, AI-capable notebooks, and professional workstations are likely to keep moving toward higher memory capacities because their workloads benefit more directly from additional RAM.
