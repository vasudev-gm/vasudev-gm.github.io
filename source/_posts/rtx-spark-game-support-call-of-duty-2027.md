### Quick Report

Microsoft says the RTX Spark platform will support hundreds of PC and AAA games from day one, but native Call of Duty support is not expected until 2027. The split shows that a broad launch catalog does not mean every major franchise will be ready at the same time.
<!-- more -->

RTX Spark is an Arm-based platform pairing an NVIDIA Blackwell graphics processor with a Grace CPU. The delayed Call of Duty support points to the additional work needed to bring a major game series to this hardware and software environment, even as other titles are expected to be available at launch.

For prospective buyers, the practical question is less about the headline game count and more about whether the specific games they play are supported natively. Microsoft and NVIDIA will need to keep expanding compatibility after launch, with Call of Duty serving as a prominent test of that effort in 2027.
