### Quick Report

A repair technician has reported a melted PSU-side 12V-2x6 connector on a system built around NVIDIA\'s $15,000 RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell Workstation Edition. The ASRock Taichi TC-1650T power supply used a native cable with an NTC temperature sensor and TempGuard protection, but the connector reportedly failed while both ends were fully seated.
<!-- more -->

The GPU reportedly survived and the damage was confined to the power-supply side. The technician claims the temperature sensor was placed on the ground side rather than the 12 V side it was intended to monitor, potentially explaining why the system did not shut down in time. The same GPU and PSU combination has reportedly seen a similar incident before, raising questions about sensor placement and connector reliability.
