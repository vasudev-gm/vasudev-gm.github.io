### Quick Report

An Intel Nova Lake-S processor with Xe3P integrated graphics is reportedly being configured to draw up to 65 watts for its iGPU. That would represent an unusually large power allowance for integrated desktop graphics and could give the platform more room for gaming and graphics workloads.
<!-- more -->

The figure remains a reported specification rather than a confirmed retail guarantee. Actual performance will depend on the final core configuration, memory bandwidth, firmware limits, and how Intel balances CPU and GPU power under sustained workloads.
