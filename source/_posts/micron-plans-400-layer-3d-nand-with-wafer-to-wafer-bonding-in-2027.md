### Quick Report

Micron has outlined plans for 400-layer 3D NAND with wafer-to-wafer bonding, a move that would push storage density significantly higher while increasing manufacturing complexity. The roadmap suggests the next generation of NAND is likely to center on more aggressive stacking and tighter process integration rather than simple die shrinks.
<!-- more -->

This kind of advancement matters because 3D NAND density directly influences SSD capacity, cost, and performance scaling across consumer and enterprise storage markets. If Micron reaches that roadmap on time, it could shape the next wave of SSD pricing and capacity planning across PCs, data centers, and embedded systems.
