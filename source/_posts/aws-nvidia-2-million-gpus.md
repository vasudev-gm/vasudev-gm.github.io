### Quick Report

AWS and NVIDIA are expanding their partnership with plans to deploy 2 million additional NVIDIA GPUs across AWS global infrastructure during 2027 and 2028. The deployment will include Blackwell Ultra, Rubin, and Rubin Ultra systems for agentic AI, scientific research, enterprise automation, and physical AI workloads.
<!-- more -->

The companies also plan to bring NVIDIA Vera CPU infrastructure to AWS, extend NVLink Fusion support to NVIDIA NVHBM, and build secure AI factories with up to 100,000 GPUs for US government workloads. AWS will integrate the platform with Nitro and Elastic Fabric Adapter, while GPU-accelerated data processing and vector indexing are expected to deliver faster analytics and AI deployment.
