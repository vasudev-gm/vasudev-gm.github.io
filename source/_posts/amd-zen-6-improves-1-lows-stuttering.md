### Quick Report

AMD Zen 6 is reportedly expected to improve 1 percent low frame rates and reduce in-game stuttering. These changes would focus on frame-time consistency rather than only increasing peak benchmark performance.
<!-- more -->

Better low-percentile results could make demanding games feel smoother, particularly during busy scenes and rapid asset streaming. Actual gains will depend on the final architecture, software support, and the workloads used to measure frame pacing.
