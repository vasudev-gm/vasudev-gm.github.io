### Quick Report

Qualcomm is broadening its top-end smartphone lineup with two new SoC families aimed at premium Android handsets. The Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 6 and Elite Extreme Gen 6 keep the emphasis on agentic AI, camera work, and heavier mobile gaming, while also giving OEMs more room to tailor flagship designs around different performance and price points.
<!-- more -->

The bigger story is how Qualcomm is separating the two tiers without abandoning its core platform strategy. Both chips remain built on the same 2 nm approach and share the Oryon CPU and Hexagon NPU foundations, but the Extreme tier is positioned as the more aggressive option for device makers chasing the highest-end AI and graphics features. That makes the launch less about a single blockbuster chip and more about giving vendors a sharper menu of flagship choices.
